Class 9 - Maths

Number Systems

Mukhya baatein

  • Rational number: p/q ke roop mein likha ja sake (q ≠ 0). Decimal expansion ya to khatam hota hai ya repeat hota hai.
  • Irrational number: p/q ke roop mein nahi likha ja sakta. Jaise √2, √3, π. Decimal expansion na khatam hota hai na repeat.
  • Real numbers: Rational aur irrational dono milkar real numbers banate hain.

Exponent ke niyam

  • aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
  • (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
  • a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0)

Denominator ko rationalise karna

  • 1/√2 = √2/2 (surd ko upar aur neeche dono taraf se gunaa karein)
Class 9 - Science

Matter in Our Surroundings

Mukhya baatein

  • Matter ke kan (particles) ke beech khaali jagah hoti hai, kan lagataar gati karte hain aur ek doosre ko aakarshit karte hain.
  • Avastha (states): Thos (solid), Drav (liquid), Gais (gas).
  • Avastha parivartan: Melting (galna), Boiling (ubalna), Evaporation (vaashpikaran), Condensation, Sublimation (jaise camphor, ice).
  • Latent heat: Avastha badalne mein lagne wali garmi, jo tapmaan nahi badhaati.
  • Diffusion: Kan ek jagah se doosri jagah jaate hain, jaise ittar ki khushboo phailna.
Class 10 - Maths

Real Numbers

Mukhya baatein

  • Euclid ka division lemma: a = bq + r, jahan 0 ≤ r < b. Ise HCF nikalne mein use karte hain.
  • Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Har composite number ko prime numbers ke gunaa ke roop mein ek hi tarah se likha ja sakta hai.
  • Irrationality ka proof: √2, √3, √5 irrational hain. Yeh contradiction se sabit kiya jaata hai.

Decimal expansion ka niyam

  • p/q ka decimal khatam hoga agar q ke prime factors sirf 2 ya 5 hain. Warna repeat hoga.
Class 10 - Science

Chemical Reactions and Equations

Mukhya baatein

  • Rasayanik samikaran mein reactants (lagne wale) aur products (bane padarth) hote hain.
  • Balancing: Dono taraf har tatv ke atom barabar hone chahiye (mass ka sanrakshan).
  • Prakar: Sanyojan (combination), Vighatan (decomposition), Vistapan (displacement), Dvi-vistapan (double displacement).
  • Oxidation: Oxygen milna ya hydrogen khona. Reduction: Oxygen khona ya hydrogen milna.

Roz ke jeevan mein

  • Rancidity: Khaane ke tel ya ghee ka oxidation se kharab hona.
  • Corrosion: Dhaatu par jang lagna, jaise lohe par jang.
Class 11 - Physics

Units and Measurement

Mukhya baatein

  • SI base units: Lambai (m), dravyaman (kg), samay (s), vidyut dhara (A), tapmaan (K), padarth ki matra (mol), prakash ki tivrata (cd).
  • Significant figures: Mapne mein vishwasniya ankon ki sankhya.
  • Dimensions: Kisi rashi ko mool rashiyon ke roop mein likhna, jaise gati = [L T⁻¹].

Trutiyan (errors)

  • Absolute error: Asli maan aur mape gaye maan ka antar.
  • Relative error: Absolute error ko asli maan se bhaag dena.
  • Percentage error: Relative error ko 100 se gunaa karna.
Class 11 - Chemistry

Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry

Mukhya baatein

  • Mole: 6.022 × 10²³ kan. Yeh Avogadro sankhya hai.
  • Molar mass: 1 mole padarth ka dravyaman (g/mol).
  • Limiting reagent: Woh reactant jo sabse pehle khatam hota hai aur utpaad ki matra tay karta hai.
  • Empirical formula: Sabse saral tatv-anupaat wala sootra.
  • Percentage composition: Har tatv ka dravyaman, poore yaugik ke dravyaman ka pratishat.
Class 12 - Physics

Electric Charges and Fields

Mukhya baatein

  • Charge ka sanrakshan: Kul charge kisi bhi nikay mein nahi badalta.
  • Quantisation: Charge hamesha electron ke charge (e) ka poornank gunaa hota hai.
  • Coulomb ka niyam: F = k q₁q₂ / r². Do charges ke beech ka bal unke gunaa ke seedhe anupaat aur doori ke varg ke ulte anupaat mein hota hai.
  • Electric field: E = F / q₀. Yeh ek test charge par lagne wala bal hai, per unit charge.

Field lines

  • Field lines positive se negative ki taraf jaati hain.
  • Do field lines kabhi ek doosre ko kaatti nahi hain.
Class 12 - Chemistry

The Solid State

Mukhya baatein

  • Amorphous: Anishchit aakar (jaise kaanch). Crystalline: Nishchit aakar aur niyamit vyavastha.
  • Unit cell: Crystal ki sabse chhoti dohraane wali ikai.
  • Packing efficiency: Unit cell mein kan kitne pratishat jagah gherte hain.
  • Defects: Schottky defect (ricti sthaan, jaise NaCl) aur Frenkel defect (kan apni jagah se visthapit).
Class 12 - History

Bricks, Beads and Bones: The Harappan Civilisation

Mukhya baatein

  • Harappan sabhyata (Indus civilisation) lagbhag 2600 se 1900 BCE ke beech ki hai, aur mukhya shehar Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal, Kalibangan aur Dholavira the.
  • Shehar ki yojana: Pakki eent, sadken aur seedhi grid, aur ghar mein neeche ki nali ka pani nikaasi.
  • Anaaj ke bhandaar (granary) aur kaam ki jagah dikhti hain.
  • Vyapaar: Mesopotamia tak samudri aur sthalmarg ka vyapaar.
  • Sroth: Puratatvik khudaai, mohar aur vastuen. Harappan lipi ab tak nahi padhi gayi hai.
